## Inputs and missing information

Yield slopes, currency correlations, momentum and volatility can be derived from existing price histories, but calling these new information would duplicate V8–V10/THESIS/location/state-retrieval work. Traded forward settlements, original overnight funding rates and dated future calendars are separately sourced potential information; availability is a hard condition. Current Final cards do not promise forward/funding inputs, and runtime internet access is restricted. Reading a signal offline during research does not make it deployable.

Preannounced FOMC calendars require no future policy decision or surprise. Conditional risk may be concentrated around a known timestamp even when the expected direction is zero. However the market may already price this risk; baseline volatility and block paths may already absorb it; a scalar variance budget may worsen CRPS or tails. Calendar count therefore remains falsifiable.
