## Executive summary (read this first)
Implemented feature_builder.py,policy_signal.py,training_data.py,engine.py,inference_adapter.py,evaluate.py and tests. Engine includes conditional_mean (multioutput ridge), conditional_volatility (ridge log squared validation residuals plus training-side moment correction), joint_generator (36-dimensional covariance shrunk20% to its diagonal, Student-t df7). Each row is a complete9-tenor x4-horizon scenario. Mean and risk are learned; no FF03 output transforms. Hyperparameters selected only using chronological validation. Full125features, ablation100features. Policy25includes lexical topics/deltas,recency,reliability,interactions,unavailable-schedule flags. Schedule flags constant during training and carry no learned contribution. Genuine futures/OIS/consensus surprises absent. No live inference training. Research adapter supports exactly9known UST tenors, horizons1..4, and posttraining asof; no production routing/fallback integration was built after performance failed. Original FF03 fallback remains available as a separate unchanged submission candidate, not as an implemented ALPHA23 production wrapper.
