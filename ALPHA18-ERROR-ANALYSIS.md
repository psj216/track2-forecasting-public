## Opportunity assessment

The strongest empirical finding is conditional dispersion/tail miscalibration, especially selected crisis episodes and stale inputs. It does not identify a new observable source that distinguishes future shocks beyond the model's existing history/text state. Simple variance/bias fitting would repeat V8/V9/CENTER and outcome selection. Joint/temporal repacking would repeat prior copula work without new predictive evidence. The evidence supports keeping FF03 unchanged and rejecting an invented alpha claim, rather than declaring that all possible short-horizon information is exhausted.
